The agent cannot move funds outside the vault and its whitelisted strategies, caps are enforced after every rebalance and owner withdraw, and no third party can push NAV down or the high-water mark (HWM) up for free to force a trip (fuzzed). One Medium: the permissionless `trip()` was all-or-nothing, so one strategy that could not exit (Aave at 100% utilization) blocked the breaker and every other exit. Fixed with a regression test on mocks and on the real Base Aave pool.

## 4. Findings

### [M-1] `trip()` / `exitAll()` revert atomically if any strategy cannot exit — **Fixed**

**Where:** `src/CircuitVault.sol` `_exitAll()` (orig. lines 329-336), reached from `trip()` (295-306) and `exitAll()` (245-248); `AaveV3UsdcAdapter.withdrawAll()` uses `pool.withdraw(usdc, type(uint256).max, vault)`, which reverts when Aave has less free liquidity than the vault's aToken balance.

**Impact:** The breaker's job is to latch and exit when value falls. Losses and Aave liquidity crunches tend to come together (bank-run, depeg). With autoExit on (default), one illiquid strategy made `trip()` revert, so `tripped` never latched, no strategy was unwound (including the lossy one that still had liquidity), and the guardian's `exitAll()` failed the same way. Only per-strategy `exitStrategy` by owner/guardian still worked, so the permissionless safety net was gone exactly when it was needed. Confidence 75 (needs a ≥ maxDrawdown loss plus an illiquid strategy at the same time).

**PoC (Confirmed):** `test_M1_tripLatchesAndExitsOthersWhenAaveIlliquid`: Aave sleeve 40k, second strategy 40k loses 10k, Aave liquidity drained. On `main` `trip()` reverts with `ERC20InsufficientBalance`; on the fix branch it latches, recovers the 30k from the other strategy, and leaves Aave retryable via `exitAll()` once liquidity returns.

**Fix:** `_exitAll()` now wraps each `withdrawAll()` in `try/catch`, emits `StrategyExitFailed(strategy, reason)` and keeps going. To stop the classic 63/64 gas-griefing of try/catch (caller sends just enough gas that the inner call fails OOG, latching a trip with no exit), the catch reverts with `ExitOutOfGas()` if less than 50k gas is left. Regression tests: the PoC above, `testFuzz_M1_tripCannotBeGasGriefed` (1000 runs, mocks), and on the Base fork `test_fork_M1_tripExitsRealAaveAndOtherStrategy`, `test_fork_M1_tripLatchesWhenAaveWithdrawReverts`, `testFuzz_fork_M1_tripCannotBeGasGriefed` (any trip that succeeds has exited every strategy).

**audit-kb:** Electisec / LiquityV2Lender, "Emergency withdraw can revert atomically when trove closure is unavailable" (medium); yAxis H-04 "Controller does not raise an error when there's insufficient liquidity" (solodit:42284); attack-vectors FP note "loop uses try/catch and continues on failure".

### [L-1] `resetTrip()` does not re-arm the breaker after a real loss — Acknowledged

**Where:** `resetTrip()` (251-255) only raises the HWM via `_checkpoint()`; nothing lowers it.
**Impact:** After a real loss, `canTrip()` stays true after reset: the agent's `rebalance` reverts `DrawdownLimitHit` and anyone can `trip()` again. The owner must widen `maxDrawdownBps` (the existing test does this) or withdraw everything and re-deposit. No fund loss; recovery is clunky and widening the limit loosens protection. PoC: `test_L1_resetTripDoesNotRearmAfterRealLoss`.
**Recommendation:** add `resetTrip(bool rebaseHwm)` that sets `highWaterMark = totalValue()` when the owner explicitly accepts the loss. audit-kb: "Stale High-Water Mark Suppresses Fees After Vault Recapitalization" (Shieldify, medium).

### [L-2] No partial exit while Aave is illiquid — Acknowledged

`withdrawAll()` asks Aave for the full balance and `rebalance` is blocked once `canTrip()`, so during a liquidity crunch nobody can pull the part that is available; other suppliers drain it first. Recommendation: let `withdrawAll()` cap at the reserve's available liquidity (Aave v3.1+ `getVirtualUnderlyingBalance`), or add an owner/guardian `exitStrategyPartial(strategy, amount)`.

### [L-3] Owner `withdraw()` blocked by `_assertCaps()` when a strategy is illiquid — Acknowledged

Withdrawing cash raises the Aave share; if Aave cannot be exited, `withdraw` reverts `ExceedsAllocationCap`. Workaround exists: owner sets the Aave cap to 100% (`setStrategy(aave, true, 10000)`) then withdraws. Document in the dapp.

### Info

- **I-1** `exitStrategy(strategy)` accepts any address (owner/guardian). The vault holds no standing approvals, and calls are `nonReentrant`, so it is harmless; consider requiring `strategies[strategy].index != 0`.
- **I-2** A listed strategy whose `totalAssets()` reverts DoSes `totalValue()` and every path using it (owner-trusted strategy; disabled strategies are never removed from the list).
- **I-3** `renounceOwnership()` (inherited) would strand funds; consider overriding it to revert.
- **I-4** With v1 (Aave only) a ≥10% drawdown is practically unreachable: Aave v3.3 records bad debt as deficit and does not haircut aToken balances. The breaker matters once a second strategy is added.

## 5. Refuted leads (checked, not issues)

- Agent leaking funds: `rebalance` only moves cash ↔ an allowed strategy; existing `testFuzz_agentCannotLeak` + invariants hold.
- Forcing a trip by donation/HWM inflation: donations raise NAV and HWM equally, `checkpoint()` cannot inflate HWM above real NAV (`testFuzz_donationCannotForceTrip`).
- Deposit erasing a drawdown: HWM += amount; percentage drawdown shrinks by design (spec).
- aToken rounding: ≤ a few wei per rebalance, agent pays gas far above any effect.
- Approvals: `forceApprove(strategy, need)` reset to 0 after each deposit; adapter's max approval is to the official Pool only.
